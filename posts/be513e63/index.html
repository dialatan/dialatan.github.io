梳理了两个视频的字幕，希望有启发~

  1. How Minimalism Can Make You More Productive - YouTube
  • One of the biggest benefits I’ve received from living a life with less has been that I’ve been able to create more. (极简主义→创造更多)
  • People get caught up in the rat race because it’s just an easier target to aim for. (in the rat race好形象呀，踩轮子的小白鼠)
  • Setting priorities when you identify what you really want to create. You need to drop every non-essential work task that doesn’t support that. (分清轻重缓急，非常重要的点，很多拖延就是因为害怕失败害怕面对困难而选择去做不那么重要和紧急的事情)
  • Be ware of booby traps.(注意力是有限的，不要被低营养的内容绑架——《注意力商人》）
  • FEELING PRODUCTIVE AND BEING PRODUCTIVE ARE DIFFERENT.(警惕自我感觉良好)
  • STAYING ORGANIZED, CREAT YOUR OWN WORKFLOW.
  • Productivity isn’t about cramming as much work as you can into one day. You’ll burn yourself out before you ever develop a routine.(破除执念，有时候一开始做某事就一定非要密集的在一天内做成，容易效率不高而且伤身，细水流长才是正道)
  • True productivity is about creating a sustainable and balanced life that you can keep up every day.（可持续发展）

    所以极简主义从扔东西开始？收拾房间打扫卫生确实是非常治愈。

  1. KEEP THEM POOR | This Is What The Richest Don’t Want You To KNOW (an illuminating interview) - YouTube

    (当时看了眼标题，点开才知道原来这位就是写《富爸爸，穷爸爸》的作者）

  • The moment you accept the paycheck, your brain goes dead. That’s the trap.
  • As long as you’re hungry, you’ll think.(穷则思变)
  • The school system is designed to teach you to be an employee, or a doctor or a lawyer, a specialist, but never about money.
  • It’s in their words and their words become flesh.(语言与思维的关系)
  • But when they say “I can’t afford it”, or “I can’t do that”, they go down. They become what they say.
  • How can I afford it? How can I do that?
  • A question opens a mind, a statement closes the mind.(去解决问题而不是预判自己不行)
  • So when you say “I can’t afford it”, your mind shuts down and you become what you say.
  • Poverty is passed on!(可怕的事实)
  • Most people only want to be right. Or they only want to be positive. Well, you can’t have that! That’s not reality!
  • A fake teacher is somebody who just wants a job and they’ll teach anything.
    And a real teacher is doing what they teach everyday.(所以自己成功了再去分享才有说服力，so why am i doing this?( ﹁ ﹁ ) ~→拖延症还未治愈，有在做事的错觉也不在乎是否有说服力( ╯□╰ ))
  • So what I say to young people is you find your game!
  • A lot of people would rather sit in the stands, than be in the game.(不要做别人生活的旁观者)
  • Our schools punish you for making mistakes.
  • And that’s why we have so many people who are so afraid of admitting they make mistakes, or are afraid of failing.(失败使人成长，成功更使人成长)
